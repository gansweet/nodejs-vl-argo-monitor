Science & Knowledge

Understand the language of life.

Biotechnology begins with a simple idea: living systems contain information, processes that information, and responds to its environment.

DNA, genes, proteins, cells and biological pathways form an interconnected system. Modern biotechnology learns to observe, modify and engineer parts of that system.

Explore Biology →
DNA

What is DNA?

DNA, or deoxyribonucleic acid, stores biological information using sequences composed primarily of four bases: A, T, C and G.

G

What is a Gene?

A gene is a region of DNA associated with a functional biological product or regulatory function. Genes participate in determining many biological traits.

◉

What is a Cell?

Cells are fundamental units of life. They contain molecular machinery that transforms energy, builds molecules and responds to signals.

⚙

What are Proteins?

Proteins are molecular machines and structural components that perform enormous numbers of biological functions.
